⚠ Important Clinical & Health Notice

  • Screening Measure: Body Mass Index (BMI) is a statistical screening measure calculated from height and weight; it is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional healthcare evaluation.
  • Muscle vs. Fat: BMI does not directly measure body fat percentage or body composition. Highly active individuals and athletes with significant muscle mass may record higher BMI scores without excess body fat.
  • Age Interpretation: Standard adult BMI categories are intended strictly for non-pregnant adults aged 18 and older. They are not interpreted in the same way for children or teenagers, who use age-and-gender-specific growth percentiles.
