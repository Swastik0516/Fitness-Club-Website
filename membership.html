Common Questions

Membership FAQ

Is there any initial joining or sign-up fee?

No, FitnessClub does not charge hidden sign-up or admin charges. What you see on the plan is what you pay.

Can I freeze my membership if I travel?

Yes, members on 6-month plans can freeze their account for up to 30 days with prior notice at our front desk.

Are locker rooms and showers included?

Yes, all active FitnessClub memberships include access to clean, modern day-use lockers and hot showers.

How do I finalize my registration?

Simply select your plan above, then complete your registration details at our front desk or via our Contact page.
